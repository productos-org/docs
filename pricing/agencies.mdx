## Studio

$$599 per month, or $$**5,990 billed annually (displayed as \$499 per month).**

Studio is for boutique agencies and product studios delivering up to 10 active client products. It gives you ProductOS-branded client previews and reports, separate AI budgets per client product, and enough capacity for a small delivery team.

### What is included

- 10 active client products
- 12,000 pooled AI credits per month
- Advanced and Frontier model access
- 8 concurrent agents
- 5 User Licenses
- Unlimited Review Licenses for clients (view and approve only)
- ProductOS-branded client previews and reports
- Separate AI budgets for every client product
- Client approvals and per-client usage controls
- Standard build concurrency

### What is not included

Studio does not include white-label delivery. Client previews, reports, and the client portal carry ProductOS branding. If you need to deliver under your own brand, upgrade to Scale.

### Add-ons

| Add-on | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Additional User License | \$79 / month |
| Additional active client product | \$39 / month |

### Who it is for

Studio fits boutique agencies building products for clients while ProductOS handles the AI-powered workflow. Clients receive Review Licenses without consuming a paid User or Collaborator License; identity is still required so approvals remain attributable and auditable.

### Switching to Scale

Scale adds white-label previews, reports, and the client portal. It also adds client-level AI credit pools with margin controls, agency-controlled top-ups, priority build concurrency, and 40 active client products. If you are delivering for clients who expect a branded experience, or you need to scale beyond 10 active client products, Scale is the upgrade.

## Scale

$$1,499 per month, or $$**14,990 billed annually (displayed as \$1,249 per month).**

Scale is for established agencies that are scaling white-label delivery across a growing client portfolio. It includes full white-label rights, 40 client products, 35,000 credits, and priority build concurrency.

### What is included

- 40 active client products
- 35,000 pooled AI credits per month
- Full Frontier model access
- 25 concurrent agents
- 15 User Licenses
- Unlimited review-only client access
- White-label previews, reports, and client portal
- Advanced permissions and audit logs
- Priority build concurrency
- Client-level AI credit pools and margin controls
- Agency-controlled top-ups
- Premium image and video models
- Dedicated onboarding and support
- Discounted Production Handoff engagements

### What white-label covers

White-label on Scale includes:

- Client previews under your branding
- Reports under your branding
- Client portal under your branding

White-label means the agency delivers under its own brand identity. The client portal, reports, and preview URLs carry the agency branding, not ProductOS branding.

### Add-ons

| Add-on | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Additional User License | \$69 / month |
| Additional active client product | \$29 / month |

### Who it is for

Scale fits agencies that are delivering production products for multiple clients and need to present a consistent brand to each one. The client credit pools and margin controls also make it practical to pass AI costs through to clients as part of a project fee, rather than absorbing them as platform overhead.

### Switching to Enterprise

Enterprise adds custom staff and product volume, multi-brand white-label controls, SSO and SCIM, multi-brand model policies and allowlists, dedicated private model routing, private infrastructure, SLA and compliance support, and custom Production Handoff terms. Contact [enterprise@productos.dev](mailto:enterprise@productos.dev) to discuss custom terms.

## Credit pooling and client budgets

AI credits pool at the workspace level on agency plans. Admins can then set separate budgets for every client product, approve top-ups per client, and decide which products may use Frontier models.

Agency-controlled top-ups on Scale and Enterprise allow the agency to purchase additional credits and allocate them to specific client products without requiring client involvement.

Review Licenses do not consume AI capacity because reviewers cannot run agents.

---

## Annual billing

Annual billing for Studio is $$5,990, priced at 10 months and billed once annually. The displayed equivalent is $$499 per month.

Annual billing for Scale is $$14,990, priced at 10 months and billed once annually. The displayed equivalent is $$1,249 per month.

Annual billing simplifies client quoting and includes two months compared with paying monthly for a full year.

## Handoff and transfer

Agencies can hand off client products to the client at any time. Ownership, billing, and administration transfer without rebuilding the project. The client continues on their own ProductOS plan, and the agency retains access only if the client invites it back.

For clients who want to fully detach from ProductOS, Production Handoff packages the code, environment, and history for independent hosting, with optional ongoing care afterward. Scale and Enterprise include discounted Production Handoff rates.
