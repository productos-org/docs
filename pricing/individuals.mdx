Individual plans cover solo makers, indie developers, and founders who are building on their own. You pay for the products you operate and nothing else.

## Hobby

**\$0 per month.**

Hobby is for curious makers, students, and anyone exploring ProductOS before committing to a paid plan. It provides the full ProductOS pipeline in a branded draft preview.

### What is included

- 1 personal draft product
- 100 AI credits per month
- Essential model access
- 1 active agent
- Full pipeline preview (Design, Develop, Build)
- Limited image and multimodal generation
- ProductOS branding on the preview

### What is not included

Hobby does not include production deploy, custom domains, Frontier model access, or the ability to operate more than one personal draft. When you are ready to ship, you upgrade to Builder or Founder.

### Switching to a paid plan

An upgrade must preserve product history, design systems, and code. The checkout flow shows the effective date and any billing adjustment before confirmation.

## Builder

$$29 per month, or $$**290 billed annually (displayed as \$24 per month).**

Builder is for solo builders, indie developers, and consultants shipping one production product. It gives you one User License, production deploy, and enough AI capacity for ongoing iteration.

### What is included

- 1 active product
- 750 AI credits per month
- Essential and Advanced model access
- 2 concurrent agents
- 1 User License
- Production deploy with custom domain and backend
- Private GitHub sync
- Standard image and multimodal models
- Frontier models available with top-ups

### Who it is for

Builder is the natural next step when you have validated an idea and are ready to put it in front of users. You get production infrastructure, your own domain, and enough AI capacity to iterate without watching the credit counter constantly.

### Switching to Founder

Founder adds two more active products, a larger credit pool, Frontier model access, the Auto Router, and one mobile release channel. If you are starting to operate a portfolio or want access to the strongest models without managing top-ups, upgrade to Founder.

## Founder

$$79 per month, or $$**790 billed annually (displayed as \$66 per month).**

Founder is for founders and serial makers operating a growing portfolio of products. It includes three active products, Frontier model access, the Auto Router, and one mobile release channel.

### What is included

- 3 active products
- 2,000 AI credits per month
- Advanced and Frontier model access
- 3 concurrent agents
- 1 User License
- Shared design systems across all three products
- ProductOS Auto Router included
- One mobile release channel (iOS and Android release operations as one channel)
- Premium image models
- AI credits shared across the founder's products
- Up to 3 free Review licenses

### Who it is for

Founder fits founders who are running more than one product in parallel and want the strongest models and automatic routing without managing individual top-ups. It also covers the mobile channel for founders who are building both a web and mobile presence.

### Annual billing

Annual billing for Founder is $$790 per year, priced at 10 months and billed once annually. The displayed equivalent is $$66 per month. The annual charge is \$790.

### Upgrade path

If you need more than three active products, more User Licenses, or agency-style client delivery, the natural next step is a team or agency plan. Contact [hello@productos.dev](mailto:hello@productos.dev) to discuss the transition.

## Switching between individual plans

Plan changes must preserve product data and show the effective date before confirmation. A downgrade cannot silently archive products or remove a live entitlement. If the destination plan has fewer product slots, archive products or select another plan before the change can take effect.

---

## Credit and concurrency details

Individual plans have one User License, so their included credit pool is used by that workspace owner across eligible products. Paid-plan included credits roll over for one billing cycle; Hobby credits do not. When credits run out, new AI builds pause until the next renewal, an approved top-up, or a plan upgrade.

Concurrency limits how many agents can run simultaneously. Hobby allows 1 agent. Builder allows 2 concurrent agents. Founder allows 3 concurrent agents. You cannot exceed the concurrency limit by purchasing additional credits; additional concurrency requires a plan upgrade.
