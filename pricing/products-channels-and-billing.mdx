ProductOS bills for the products you operate, the people who work on them, and the AI capacity they use. This page covers how products are defined, how channels relate to products, and how billing changes when you add, remove, or transfer products.

## What is an active product

A product becomes active in ProductOS once it has a live domain, backend, or app store release. Until then, it is a draft and does not count against your product allowance.

A product is a digital experience for your end users. It has a name, a design system, a codebase, a backend, and one or more delivery channels.

### Product identity and lineage

A product retains its identity and full history across:

- A fork or clone
- A new GitHub repository
- A new domain
- A transfer to another workspace

Lineage, including the history, design system, and code links, persists. This means you can restructure your repository or move a product to a different workspace without rebuilding it.

## Channels

A channel is a delivery surface for your product. The same product identity can be delivered across multiple channels, all sharing the design system, backend, context, and code.

| Channel | Description | Included in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Web | Custom domain, production hosting, and backend | Paid plans with an active-product allowance |
| Web preview | Draft preview with ProductOS branding | Hobby |
| Mobile | iOS and Android release operations | Add-on in most plans |
| Desktop | Desktop packaging and release operations | Add-on in most plans |
| AI intelligence | Credit pool across every ProductOS stage | Included by plan |

Paid plans include the web production channel. Hobby includes a branded draft preview but not a production deployment. Mobile and desktop are add-ons in most paid plans. Founder includes one mobile release channel.

### Channel pricing

| Channel | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Mobile release operations | \+\$29 per product per month |
| Desktop release operations | \+\$15 per product per month |

Channels are billed per product. If you have three active products and add a mobile channel to all three, you pay \$29 times three, or \$87 per month.

### Adding a channel

Adding a channel updates the product entitlement after checkout confirmation. Monthly workspaces charge the prorated add-on immediately; annual workspaces record the prorated obligation for the annual true-up.

### Founder mobile channel

Founder includes one mobile release channel. This means you can add mobile release operations to one product without paying the add-on fee. Additional products or additional channels are billed at the standard add-on rate.

## Monthly billing

Published self-service plans bill monthly by default. You pay for the plan and active add-ons on the workspace billing date. Enterprise terms are defined in the executed order.

## Annual billing

Annual billing is priced at 10 times the monthly price and charged once per year. You pay the full annual amount upfront and receive two months included compared with paying monthly for a full year.

The displayed monthly equivalent is rounded to the nearest dollar. The annual total is exact.

| Plan | Monthly | Annual total | Displayed equivalent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Builder | \$29 | \$290 | \$24 / month |
| Founder | \$79 | \$790 | \$66 / month |
| Startup | \$399 | \$3,990 | \$333 / month |
| Business | \$1,199 | \$11,990 | \$999 / month |
| Studio | \$599 | \$5,990 | \$499 / month |
| Scale | \$1,499 | \$14,990 | \$1,249 / month |

Annual billing is available on published paid plans. Contracted mid-year additions are tracked as true-ups under the workspace's order terms.

## License additions and reductions

### Adding a license

Adding a User or Collaborator License creates a prorated obligation for the remainder of the current term. Monthly workspaces charge that addition immediately; annual workspaces record it for the annual true-up. After renewal, the license is billed for the full term.

### Removing a license

Removing a license takes effect at your next renewal. Your team keeps access through the end of what you already paid for. You do not lose the value of the paid term.

### Released licenses

A paid license released mid-term stays available for reassignment to someone else for the remainder of that term. You can reassign it without losing the billing benefit of what you already paid.

### Reassignment

Reassignment is audited. There is a reasonable cooldown between reassignments to prevent abuse. Concurrency and device controls enforce the named-user policy.

### Activating another product

You can activate another product while the workspace remains within its included allowance. If the allowance is full, upgrade to a plan with more active products or purchase the published add-on where available. Checkout must show the price and effective date before activation.

### Archiving a product

Archiving stops ProductOS production operation for that product and frees its active-product slot immediately while preserving its history. A lower plan price or contracted product-pack reduction takes effect according to the change shown at checkout or the executed order.

## True-ups

Annual workspaces settle contracted mid-year license or product-pack additions at renewal. The true-up covers the partial period from the addition date through the end of the annual term; the renewed subscription then includes the full annual amount.

For example, a User License added 60 days into a 365-day annual term produces a prorated obligation for the remaining 305 days, settled under the annual true-up.

## Archive and reactivation

You can archive a product at any time. Archiving frees its active-product slot while keeping its full history, design system, and code links intact. The product can be reactivated at any time without rebuilding.

Archiving is useful when a product is not currently in active operation but you want to preserve its history and be able to reactivate it later.

## ProductOS operation vs Production Handoff

ProductOS charges for operating products in production. GitHub sync, export, and fork are portability features, not billing switches. You can connect your GitHub, fork a repository, or re-import at any time without changing your bill.

When a product is fully detached from ProductOS, you have two options:

**Continue on ProductOS**: The product remains on your plan and you pay for its active-product slot.

**Production Handoff**: A separately priced engagement packages the code, environment, and history for independent hosting, with optional ongoing care afterward. This is for products that will no longer operate on ProductOS.

Scale and Enterprise include discounted Production Handoff rates. Contact [hello@productos.dev](mailto:hello@productos.dev) for Production Handoff pricing.
