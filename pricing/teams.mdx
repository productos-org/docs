## Startup

\$399 per month, or \$**3,990 billed annually (displayed as \$333 per month).**

Startup is for early-stage teams bringing their first product portfolio to market. It gives you five active products, pooled AI credits, five named members, and the core admin controls needed to coordinate a small team.

### What is included

- 5 active products
- 8,000 pooled AI credits per month
- Advanced and Frontier model access
- 5 concurrent agents
- 5 named members: 3 User Licenses, 2 Collaborator Licenses, unlimited Review Licenses
- Shared design systems across products
- Per-member AI usage limits
- Admin-approved AI top-ups
- Roles and approval workflows
- Auto Router with member limits

### Add-ons

You can add licenses and products as your team grows:

| Add-on | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Additional User License | \$59 / month |
| Additional Collaborator License | \$15 / month |
| Additional active product | \$29 / month |

### Who it is for

Startup fits teams of up to five builders who are shipping their first products. If you need to add more User Licenses, more products, or higher AI capacity, Business covers that without requiring a custom engagement.

### Switching to Business

Business adds 20 products, 25,000 pooled credits, 15 agents, 25 named members, model-level cost and quality analytics, department and product budgets, and credits that roll over for one billing cycle. If you are adding team members, managing multiple products across a department, or need visibility into which models are driving costs, Business is the upgrade.

- 25,000 pooled AI credits per month

- Advanced permissions
- Model-level cost and quality analytics

- Included credits roll over for one billing cycle

| Add-on | Price |

## Credit pooling

On team plans, AI credits pool at the workspace level. This means all members share one credit balance. Admins can set budgets by product, department, or individual member, and decide which products may use Frontier models.

Review Licenses do not consume AI capacity because reviewers cannot run agents.

Included credits on paid plans roll over for one billing cycle. Purchased top-up credits follow the terms shown at purchase.

---

## Annual billing

Annual billing for Startup is \$3,990, priced at 10 months and billed once annually. The displayed equivalent is \$333 per month.

Annual billing for Business is \$11,990, priced at 10 months and billed once annually. The displayed equivalent is \$999 per month.

Annual billing simplifies budgeting and includes two months compared with paying monthly for a full year.

---

## Adding and removing licenses

Adding a User or Collaborator License is prorated for the rest of your current billing term. Removing a license takes effect at your next renewal, so your team keeps access through what you already paid for.

A paid license released mid-term stays available for reassignment to someone else for the remainder of that term.

Reassignment is auditable, with a reasonable cooldown and concurrency and device controls to prevent credential sharing.
